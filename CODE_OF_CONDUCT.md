# Meridian Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Meridian community a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

Nos comprometemos a que la participación en la comunidad de Meridian sea respetuosa, constructiva y libre de acoso para todas las personas, sin importar su origen, identidad, nivel de experiencia o función.

## Expected Behavior

Participants should communicate with empathy, assume good intent while addressing impact, give specific and actionable feedback, welcome different technical perspectives, and protect confidential or private information. Disagreement is expected in serious engineering work; disrespect is not.

Las personas participantes deben comunicarse con empatía, asumir buena intención sin ignorar el impacto, ofrecer feedback específico y accionable, recibir perspectivas técnicas diferentes y proteger la información confidencial o privada. El desacuerdo forma parte del trabajo de ingeniería serio; la falta de respeto, no.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, credential or secret publication, attempts to reconstruct private engine code, sexualized conduct in public or private community spaces, and sustained disruption of technical discussion.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Decisions will be proportionate to the behavior and documented when appropriate.

## Reporting

For urgent safety or security concerns, follow `SECURITY.md`. For community conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through the repository's private contact channel rather than opening a public issue with sensitive details.

## Scope

This code applies to the Meridian repository, Issues, Discussions, pull requests, events, and community spaces when a person is representing or participating in Meridian.
